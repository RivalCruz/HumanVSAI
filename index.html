About us

A learning game that helps people pause, inspect, compare, and verify before trusting digital content.

Our purpose

Build stronger critical-thinking habits

is it AI? challenges players to decide whether text, images, and videos were created by people or generated with artificial intelligence. The goal is not to turn players into automatic AI detectors. It is to encourage careful observation, healthy doubt, evidence checking, and responsible sharing.

Think before you trust
01

Observe

Look at details, consistency, context, language, motion, and visual quality.

02

Question

Ask who created the content, why it exists, and what might be missing.

03

Verify

Compare claims with reliable sources instead of trusting a single clue.

04

Reflect

Read the explanation after every round and improve your reasoning.

Responsible-use note

Human judgement and automated AI detectors can both be wrong. Important claims should always be checked using multiple reliable sources.

Privacy

is it AI? stores only the player name in the current browser. Quiz content is requested from the project API, and the frontend does not require an account.

Accessibility

The interface supports keyboard navigation, clear focus states, readable contrast, captions when provided by the media, and responsive layouts.
